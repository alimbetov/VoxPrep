# Content audio role policy

## Purpose

Audio belongs to an exact immutable `ContentRevision`, not to `Vertical`, `LearningGoal`, `LearningTrack` or `LearningCollection`.

The same universal vertical engine therefore works for Java Interview, Medical English, Banking English and future verticals without product-specific audio code.

## Content type policy

| Content type | Audio role | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `VOCABULARY` | `pronunciation` | `OPTIONAL` |
| `PHRASE` | `pronunciation` | `RECOMMENDED` |
| `INTERVIEW` | `question` | `RECOMMENDED` |
| `INTERVIEW` | `sampleAnswer` | `RECOMMENDED` |
| `TOPIC` | `narration` | `OPTIONAL` |
| `SHADOWING` | `shadowing` | `REQUIRED` |

Semantics:

- `OPTIONAL`: publication is valid without the role.
- `RECOMMENDED`: publication is valid without the role, but ADMIN validation reports a warning.
- `REQUIRED`: publication must fail closed until a READY audio asset exists.

## Interview

`INTERVIEW` deliberately has two independent tracks:

```text
Interview ContentRevision rN
├── question text
│   └── role=question       -> FileAsset Q
└── sample answer text
    └── role=sampleAnswer   -> FileAsset A
```

The question and sample answer may be generated by separate TTS jobs, use different voices, and be regenerated independently as new immutable assets. They are not represented by one ambiguous `audioAssetId`.

## Publication invariants

For every exact `ContentRevision`:

1. `(contentCode, contentType, revision, audioRole)` identifies at most one audio binding.
2. The role must be allowed for the content type.
3. The referenced asset must belong to the same publication snapshot.
4. The referenced asset must have `mediaType` beginning with `audio/`.
5. `REQUIRED` roles must be present and READY before publication.
6. `RECOMMENDED` roles produce warnings only.
7. `OPTIONAL` roles produce neither errors nor warnings when absent.
8. Signed URLs, tokens and access-grant expiry never enter immutable publication identity.

## Backend authoring flow

```text
Admin edits ContentRevision
       ↓
select/generate audio role
       ↓
tts_job or upload
       ↓
FileAsset READY
       ↓
ContentAsset binding(role, assetId)
       ↓
publication validation
       ↓
PublicationSnapshot
```

For `SHADOWING`, publication is blocked when the required role is missing or not READY.

For `INTERVIEW`, ADMIN should show two independent controls:

```text
Question audio       [Generate TTS] [Upload]
Sample answer audio  [Generate TTS] [Upload]
```

Missing roles are warnings, not publication blockers.

## Flutter boundary

Flutter consumes stable role-aware asset bindings from the CURRENT publication and resolves them through `AssetResolver`.

```text
CURRENT ContentRevision
       ↓
role
       ↓
assetId
       ↓
AssetResolver
       ↓
verified local file
       ↓
AudioPlaybackEngine
```

The client must not hard-code a single generic `promptAudio` role for all content types.

For `INTERVIEW`, UI exposes separate actions for question and sample-answer playback. Role-specific playback state/progress must not be conflated accidentally when role-aware resume semantics are introduced.

## Compatibility

Existing `hasAudio` projections may remain temporarily as a coarse compatibility/read-model flag (`true` means at least one audio binding exists). It is not the source of truth for role selection.

The source of truth is the exact publication `ContentAssetRef.role` binding.
